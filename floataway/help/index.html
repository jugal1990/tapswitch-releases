FloatAway common questions

FloatAway opens apps from Oppo's Smart Sidebar full screen, in one tap. Here are the questions people ask most.

  1. What about files I share from one app to another?
  2. What happens to apps I already have open?
  3. What happens when the 14 days end?
  4. I uninstalled FloatAway and shared files still open full screen. How do I undo it?

What about files I share from one app to another?

They open full screen too. If you would rather they did not, turn it off with the switch on FloatAway's home screen.

What happens to apps I already have open?

They stay where they are. FloatAway only changes how an app opens.

What happens when the 14 days end?

FloatAway stops, and sidebar apps open in the small window again, the way your phone did before. Shared files go back too. It asks you to buy it, and if you do not, you owe nothing.

I uninstalled FloatAway and shared files still open full screen. How do I undo it?

The sharing switch is a phone setting, so it stays after FloatAway is gone. Reinstall FloatAway, run the setup again, and switch it off on FloatAway's home screen. Then you can uninstall it.

Try it on your own phone for 14 days, free. Setting it up takes under five minutes.
