What about files I share from one app to another?

They open full screen too. If you would rather they did not, turn it off with the switch on FloatAway's home screen.

What happens to apps I already have open?

They stay where they are. FloatAway only changes how an app opens.

What happens when the 14 days end?

FloatAway stops, and sidebar apps open in the small window again, the way your phone did before. Shared files go back too. Your sidebar folders are removed, and if you buy, you set them up again. It asks you to buy it, and if you do not, you owe nothing.

I uninstalled FloatAway and shared files still open full screen. How do I undo it?

The sharing switch is a phone setting, so it stays after FloatAway is gone. Reinstall FloatAway, run the setup again, and switch it off on FloatAway's home screen. Then you can uninstall it.

What are Sidebar folders?

They put up to three folders from your phone on the Smart Sidebar as buttons. Tap a button and the folder opens full screen. The feature has a BETA tag because it is new and still being polished.

Why does it say "Can't use this folder"?

Android blocks some folders, like Download and your phone's main storage. Pick a folder inside it instead.

Why does it say "Connect to Wi-Fi first"?

Adding or clearing a folder needs Wi-Fi. Connect, then try again.

Why does FloatAway ask me to add a folder myself?

Sometimes an add does not finish. FloatAway then shows the steps: open the sidebar, tap Edit, find the folder, tap its plus sign, then tap Done. It also sends me a short report, without the folder's name, so I can fix it.

How do I remove a folder?

On FloatAway's home screen, tap its slot, then tap Clear this slot. Its button leaves the sidebar.

What if I delete or rename a folder?

Its button tells you the folder is gone and opens Sidebar folders, so you can pick a new one.
