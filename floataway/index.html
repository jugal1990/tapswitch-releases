Setting it up

You set it up once, on the phone itself. You do not need a computer or a cable.

  1. Install the file

    Your phone asks whether to allow installs from your browser. Say yes, then open FloatAway.

  2. Turn on Developer options

    FloatAway guides you through it. You only do this once.

  3. Turn on Wireless debugging

    You need Wi-Fi for this step. Your phone shows six digits, and you type them into the FloatAway notification. FloatAway turns Wireless debugging back off when it is done. Your phone may close and reopen FloatAway once. Nothing is lost.

  4. Let it run in the background

    This keeps sidebar apps opening full screen. FloatAway shows one quiet notification while it is on.

Common questions

What about files I share from one app to another?

They open full screen too. If you would rather they did not, turn it off with the switch on FloatAway's home screen.

What happens to apps I already have open?

They stay where they are. FloatAway only changes how an app opens.

What happens when the 14 days end?

FloatAway asks you to buy it. If you do not, you owe nothing.

I uninstalled FloatAway and shared files still open full screen. How do I undo it?

The sharing switch is a phone setting, so it stays after FloatAway is gone. Reinstall FloatAway, run the setup again, and switch it off on FloatAway's home screen. Then you can uninstall it.
