    Setting it up takes about five minutes

    You do everything on the phone, without a computer or cable.

    1. Install the file

      Your phone asks whether to allow installs from your browser. Say yes, then open TapSwitch.

    2. Unlock a hidden menu, once

      You tap your phone’s version number seven times. TapSwitch opens the right screen and tells you which row to tap.

    3. Turn on Wireless debugging for a minute

      You need Wi-Fi for this step. Your phone shows six digits.

      Type them into the TapSwitch notification. This gives TapSwitch the one permission it needs to change your tap card. TapSwitch then turns Wireless debugging off. On some phones the app reinstalls itself once. Nothing is lost.

    4. Pick your two cards

      Your access card app and your pay app.

    What TapSwitch can and cannot do

    Change your default tap card
    It changes only this setting, so the right card answers the reader.
    See which app is on screen
    So it knows when Google Wallet or your access card app is open.
    Reverse mode on Oppo only: Display over other apps
    A tiny invisible window, so your phone does not pause TapSwitch while your door app opens. The same window tells TapSwitch when a finger touches the screen, never where. That lets it switch to your door card a moment before your door app opens from Recent Apps. On Oppo, setup may switch it on. TapSwitch uses it only in Reverse mode.
    Check for updates once a day
    There is no account to sign in to.
    Never sees your cards
    It cannot read card numbers, payments or door logs. It only picks which app answers.
