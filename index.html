    Which phones it works on

    Oppo ColorOS phones with Android 16 or newer.

    It is built and used daily on Oppo ColorOS. It should work on any phone with Android 16 or newer, but no others have been tested yet. The 14-day free trial is how you find out if it works on yours.

    Setting it up takes about five minutes

    You do everything on the phone, without a computer or cable.

    1. Install the file

      Your phone asks whether to allow installs from your browser. Say yes, then open TapSwitch.

    2. Unlock a hidden menu, once

      You tap your phone's version number seven times. TapSwitch opens the right screen and tells you which row to tap.

    3. Turn on Wireless debugging for a minute

      You need Wi-Fi for this step. Turn on Wireless debugging, then type the six digits TapSwitch shows you in a notification. This lets TapSwitch give itself the one permission it needs to change your tap card. It switches Wireless debugging back off when it is done. On some phones the app then reinstalls itself once; nothing is lost.

    4. Pick your two cards

      Your access card app and your pay app.

    What TapSwitch can and cannot do

    Change your default tap card
    This is the only phone setting it changes, so the right card answers the reader.
    See which app is on screen
    So it knows when Google Wallet or your access card app is open.
    Check for updates once a day
    There is no account to sign in to.
    Never sees your cards
    It cannot read card numbers, payments or door logs. It only picks which app answers.
