Why does my access card stop working when Google Wallet is set up?

An Android phone offers only one tap card at a time, called the default. When Google Wallet becomes the default, door readers get Google Wallet instead of your access card, so the door stays shut.

Make the access card the default and the card machine at the shop stops seeing Google Wallet instead. Without help, you end up switching back and forth by hand every day.

How do I switch the default tap card by hand?

You can do it for free in your phone's settings:

  1. Open Settings and search for contactless.
  2. Open Contactless payments. Some phones call it Tap and pay.
  3. Pick your access card before you reach the office door.
  4. Pick Google Wallet again before you pay.

If your phone offers a setting to use the default unless another payment app is open, turn it on and try that first. Some phones, including the Oppo Find X9, do not have it.

What does TapSwitch actually do?

TapSwitch does the switching for you. Your access card stays the default all day. When you open Google Wallet, TapSwitch hands the tap over to it. When you close Google Wallet, the access card comes back.

Can Google Wallet be the default instead?

Yes. Turn on Reverse mode in the app. Google Wallet then stays the default, and your access card takes over only while its app is open.
