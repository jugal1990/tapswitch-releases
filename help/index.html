Why does my access card stop working when Google Wallet is set up?

An Android phone offers one tap card at a time: the default. If Google Wallet is the default, door readers get Google Wallet, not your access card, so the door stays shut.

If you make the access card the default, the shop card machine stops seeing Google Wallet. Without help, you switch back and forth by hand every day.

How do I switch the default tap card by hand?

You can do it for free in your phone’s settings:

  1. Open Settings and search for contactless.
  2. Open Contactless payments. Some phones call it Tap and pay.
  3. Pick your access card before you reach the office door.
  4. Pick Google Wallet again before you pay.

If your phone has a setting to use the default unless another payment app is open, turn it on first. Some phones, including the Oppo Find X9, do not have it.

What does TapSwitch actually do?

TapSwitch does the switching for you. Your access card stays the default all day. When you open Google Wallet, TapSwitch hands the tap over to it. When you close Google Wallet, the access card comes back.

You never open Settings again.

Can Google Wallet be the default instead?

Yes. Turn on Reverse mode in the app. Google Wallet then stays the default, and your access card takes over only while its app is open.

Why does the Oppo Access Card app ask to be the default?

In Reverse mode, Google Wallet is the default. Since 3.3.0, Oppo’s Access Card app shows no pop-up when you open it from a dark screen. Tap “Set it up” on the home screen once, and the pop-up stays away when your phone is awake too.

If a pop-up still shows, leave it on screen and tap the reader. TapSwitch has already switched to your access card, so the door opens.
